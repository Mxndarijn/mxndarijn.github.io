Flapjaw privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Flapjaw is a mobile game made by Mxndarijn, an independent developer in the Netherlands. This page explains what data the game uses, why, and what you can do about it.

The short version

  • You play without an account. The game never asks for your name, email address, phone number, contacts or location.
  • Your scores go to the online leaderboards under a name you choose yourself.
  • The game shows an ad only when you choose to watch one, to revive after a crash. Those ads come from Google AdMob.
  • Nothing is sold, and there is no ad that plays without you asking for it.

Data kept on your device

Your progress is saved on your phone: coins, best score, the items you own and use, the rooms you have met, your settings and the leaderboard name you picked. This save stays on the device and is removed when you delete the game.

Leaderboards

When you finish a run, the game sends it to the leaderboard server so it can appear on the daily and all-time boards. The first time, the game creates a random player ID for your device. No email or password is involved.

For each run the server stores:

  • the random player ID and the leaderboard name you chose;
  • the score, the coins collected and the date and time of the run;
  • the run itself (its seed, the moments you tapped and whether you revived), so the server can replay it and check that the score is real, and so other players can watch it;
  • the look you played with (flyer, world, trail, portal and music), shown when someone watches the run.

Other players can see your leaderboard name, your score, your look and a replay of your best run. Please do not use your real name as a leaderboard name if you do not want it to be public.

The leaderboards run on Supabase, on servers in London, United Kingdom. As with any online service, the server sees your IP address while your phone talks to it. The legal basis for this processing is the legitimate interest in running fair leaderboards that you take part in by playing.

Leaderboard data is kept for as long as the leaderboards exist, or until you ask for it to be removed.

Ads

Flapjaw has one kind of ad: a rewarded ad that you can choose to watch to revive after a crash. You can always skip it. Ads are provided by Google AdMob.

To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use data from your device, such as your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with the ad. Google is responsible for how it uses that data; see how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's privacy policy.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before any ad is requested. You can refuse, and the game plays the same: you simply get no ad revives. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings > Ad choices. On iPhone, Apple's own tracking prompt also applies.

You can also reset or switch off your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

What the game does not do

  • It does not collect your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location.
  • It does not use analytics or tracking tools of its own.
  • It does not sell personal data.

Children

Flapjaw is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has sent data through the game, contact the address below and it will be removed.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the leaderboard data that belongs to you. Send an email with your leaderboard name and roughly when you last played, so the right entries can be found. If you live in the EU or the UK you also have the right to object to this processing and to complain to your data protection authority (in the Netherlands, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Changes

If the game starts using data differently, this page is updated and the date at the top changes.
